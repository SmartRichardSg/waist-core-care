⚠️ Shoulder recovery
  • Keep the right shoulder relaxed; no overhead reaching.
  • No push-ups, planks, lifting or vigorous arm movements.
  • For the standing exercise, hold a stable support with the comfortable hand only if needed.
  • Stop if you get shoulder pain, sharp pain, dizziness or unusual discomfort.
  • Follow your doctor's or physiotherapist's restrictions if they are more limiting.
